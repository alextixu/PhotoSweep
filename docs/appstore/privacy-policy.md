# PhotoSweep Privacy Policy · 隱私權政策

_Last updated · 最後更新：2026-10-08_

## English

PhotoSweep does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Photo library access.** PhotoSweep asks for access to your photo library so it can show your photos and videos on your device. Photos are read only on your device and are never uploaded or sent anywhere.
- **What the app saves.** To remember your progress, PhotoSweep saves the identifiers of photos you kept or moved to the in-app trash, plus simple statistics (number of photos deleted and space freed). This file stays in the app's own storage on your device and is removed when you delete the app. You can erase it anytime in *Settings → Erase all app data*.
- **Deleting photos.** Photos are deleted only when you empty the trash and confirm the iOS dialog. iOS keeps deleted photos in *Recently Deleted* for 30 days.
- **No network, no tracking.** PhotoSweep makes no network connections and contains no analytics, advertising, or third-party SDKs. It does not track you across apps or websites.
- **Children.** PhotoSweep does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## 中文

PhotoSweep 不收集、不儲存、也不分享任何個人資料。

- **照片圖庫存取**：PhotoSweep 需要存取你的照片圖庫，才能在你的裝置上顯示照片與影片。照片只在你的裝置上讀取，不會上傳或傳送到任何地方。
- **App 儲存的內容**：為了記住整理進度，PhotoSweep 會儲存你保留或放進 App 垃圾桶的照片識別碼，以及簡單的統計（刪除張數與釋放空間）。這個檔案只存在你裝置上的 App 資料中，刪除 App 時會一併移除；也可以隨時在「設定 → 清除所有 App 資料」清除。
- **刪除照片**：只有在你清空垃圾桶並在 iOS 確認視窗中同意後，照片才會被刪除；iOS 會在「最近刪除」保留 30 天。
- **不連網、不追蹤**：PhotoSweep 不進行任何網路連線，沒有分析、廣告或第三方 SDK，也不會跨 App 或網站追蹤你。
- **兒童**：PhotoSweep 不會刻意收集任何人的資料，包括兒童。
- **政策變更**：若本政策有變動，新版本會公布在此網址並更新日期。
