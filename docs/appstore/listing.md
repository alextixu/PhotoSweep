Swipe to clean up your photos

Go through your library one photo at a time. Swipe right to keep, left to trash. Nothing is deleted until you empty the trash.

**Keywords** (100 characters max, comma-separated)
photo,cleaner,swipe,delete,storage,gallery,camera roll,organize,declutter,screenshots,cleanup

**Description**
PhotoSweep makes cleaning up your photo library quick and even a little fun.

Photos appear one at a time, starting with your newest. Swipe right to keep a photo, swipe left to put it in the trash. Double-tap to add it to Favorites. Tap to see it full screen.

NOTHING IS DELETED BY ACCIDENT
Swiping left only moves a photo to PhotoSweep's own trash. Restore any photo with one tap. When you're ready, empty the trash and confirm in the iOS dialog. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted for 30 days, just like in the Photos app.

PICK WHAT TO REVIEW

• Screenshots, videos, favorites, all photos, or a random mix
• Progress is remembered between sessions

MADE FOR iPHONE AND iPAD
• Full-screen preview with pinch to zoom; videos play inline
• Undo your last decision, even on the last photo
• See how much space you've freed

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
PhotoSweep has no account, no ads, no analytics and no network connection. Your photos never leave your device.

Available in English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese.

## 送審備註（App Review Information → Notes）

PhotoSweep needs access to the photo library; please allow Full Access when prompted. Swipe right to keep a photo and left to move it to the in-app trash. Photos are only deleted when the user taps "Delete N Items" in the Trash screen and confirms the system dialog. The app makes no network requests and collects no data.
